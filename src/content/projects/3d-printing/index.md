Getting into 3D printing has been a fun way to bring digital ideas into the physical world. Whether it's printing functional parts, decorative pieces, or just experimenting with new designs, there's something satisfying about watching a model take shape layer by layer.

## Printer

I'm printing on an **Ender 3 Neo** by Creality. It's a reliable entry-level FDM printer with a good community and plenty of upgrade paths. The bed-leveling with the CR Touch probe makes getting a good first layer much more consistent, and the all-metal extruder helps with more demanding filaments down the road.

## Filament

My primary filament is **Overture PLA**. It's easy to work with, produces clean prints, and comes in a wide range of colours. PLA is a great starting point — it doesn't require an enclosure, prints at lower temperatures, and handles most everyday prints without issue.
